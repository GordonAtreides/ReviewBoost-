How it works

1) Connect tools

Hook up your CRM/job app (Jobber/ServiceM8/Zapier) and add your Google/Trustpilot links.

2) Set the cadence

Choose trigger timing (immediate/next day) and channels (SMS, Email, WhatsApp). Templates are policy-safe.

3) Collect & learn

Requests go out, reviews roll in, and the dashboard shows performance and compliance checks.
