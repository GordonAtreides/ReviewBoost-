FAQ

Is this compliant with Google/Trustpilot policies?
Yes. We never gate reviews or incentivise positive sentiment. Customers always see public review links and a private feedback option in parallel.
Can my team ask in person?
Absolutely. Use our QR/NFC tap cards and staff prompts so customers can review in under 60 seconds.
What data do you store?
Minimal contact info (email/phone), timestamps, delivery/click events, and public review links. You control retention and can export/delete at any time.
